1:1 coaching

Want it fully personalised? Get Mitch in your corner

A Hyrox race is eight stations, run in order, each one setting up the next. 1:1 coaching works the same way — built around you, adjusted every week.

01
Assessment

A real look at where you're starting from

A call to go through your training history, current fitness, schedule, and what you're racing — or working — toward. No template questionnaire, just a conversation.

02
Programming

A program built around your actual week

Running, strength, and station-specific work laid out for the days and equipment you actually have — gym, home, or hotel room.

03
Check-ins

Weekly feedback, not a set-and-forget plan

Form checks on your lifts, splits on your running, and a program that shifts when your week does — not the other way around.

04
Race prep

Pacing and strategy heading into race day

Station transitions, roxzone pacing, and a taper that has you peaking on the day — not two weeks before it.
