Path 01 — Rolling hybrid group program

Lovelock Performance Crew

Structured programming. Proven framework. Training community.

The Lovelock Performance Crew is for athletes who want structured HYROX and hybrid programming without needing fully individualised 1:1 coaching. The program combines running, strength, threshold, conditioning, HYROX-specific training and race-specific work.

Your training continually progresses, allowing you to build fitness and strength throughout the year rather than constantly starting from scratch for every race.

Who is it for?

  • Preparing for your first HYROX

  • Looking to improve your running

Why a rolling program?

Your next race might be four weeks away, or six months away. Either way, you shouldn't need to completely restart your training every time you enter a new event. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to have done a HYROX before?

No. The programs are suitable for athletes across different levels of experience.

Is the Performance Crew individualised?

No. The Crew follows the Lovelock Performance framework and provides scalable options for different abilities. If you want your entire program built around your individual strengths, weaknesses and race calendar, 1:1 coaching is the better option.

What if I'm a beginner?

That's completely fine. You don't need to be an elite athlete to join the Crew. The goal is to progressively develop your fitness, strength and ability.

What if I'm already an experienced HYROX athlete?

The programming is designed to develop advanced hybrid performance qualities and includes race-specific work. If you need highly individualised programming around a specific performance goal, 1:1 coaching is recommended.

How is the program delivered?

Training is delivered through FITR, giving you access to your sessions, instructions and exercise demonstrations.

Can I join if I have a race coming up?

Yes. The Crew is a rolling program, so you don't have to wait for a new training block to begin. If you have a specific race and want your entire preparation individually structured around it, consider 1:1 coaching.

What does 1:1 coaching cost?

1:1 coaching is $125 per week. This includes personalised programming, weekly check-in calls, ongoing communication and individualised adjustments throughout your training.

How do I apply for 1:1 coaching?

Complete the application form and Mitch will review your details and get in touch regarding the next steps.

Can I switch from the Crew to 1:1?

Yes. If your goals become more specific or you decide you want individualised coaching, you can move into 1:1 coaching.
